# Free cash flow

Source: Investment Analysis And Portfolio Management.pdf, pages 315 and
343.

## Formula

free_cash_flow = cash_flow_from_operations - capital_expenditures
                 + disposition_of_property_and_equipment

Variants named on the same pages:

- free_cash_flow = cash_flow_from_operations - capital_expenditures
- conservative: also subtract dividends

## Symbols

- cash_flow_from_operations: from the statement of cash flows.
- capital_expenditures: an investing expenditure.
- disposition_of_property_and_equipment: a divestment source of cash.

## Quote

Page 315: "some analysts only subtract capital expenditures, and those who are
conservative also subtract dividends"
