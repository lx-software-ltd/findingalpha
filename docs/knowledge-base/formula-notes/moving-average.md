# Moving average

Source: BKM - Investment and Portfolio Management - Edition 9.pdf,
page 429.

## Formula

moving_average(n) = mean of the closing prices over the previous n
periods

Signal: bullish when the price crosses above the moving average from
below, and a sell when it falls below.

The two popular lengths are 200 days and 53 weeks.

The book states this in words. The formula above is written from it.

## Symbols

- n: the number of periods averaged.

## Quote

"When the market price breaks through the moving average line from
below ... it is taken as a bullish signal"

"Two popular measures are 200-day and 53-week moving averages."
