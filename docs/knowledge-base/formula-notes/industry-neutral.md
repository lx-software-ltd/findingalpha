# Industry-neutral alphas

Source: Active Portfolio Management, Grinold & Kahn.pdf, page 390.

## Formula

alpha_neutral(n) = alpha(n) - industry_average_alpha(industry of n)

The industry average is capitalisation-weighted.

The book states this in words. The formula above is written from it.

## Symbols

- alpha(n): the forecast for stock n.
- industry_average_alpha: the capitalisation-weighted mean alpha of the
  stocks in that industry.

## Quote

"Calculate the (capitalization-weighted) alpha for each industry, then
subtract the industry average alpha from each alpha in that industry."
