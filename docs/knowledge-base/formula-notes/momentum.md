# Price momentum

Sources: BKM - Investment and Portfolio Management - Edition 9.pdf,
page 391. Lectures on Financial Economics, Antonio Mele.pdf, page 222.

## Rule

Rank stocks by their past return. Stocks with the best recent returns
tend to keep outperforming, and the worst keep underperforming, over
holding periods of 3 to 12 months.

past_return = price_now / price_12_months_ago - 1

Neither book skips the most recent month.

The books state this in words. The formula above is written from them.

## Symbols

- price_now, price_12_months_ago: closing prices at the two dates.

## Quote

BKM page 391: "using 3- to 12-month holding periods ... a momentum effect
in which good or bad recent performance of particular stocks continues
over time"

Mele page 222: "Stocks with the highest returns in the previous twelve
months will outperform in the next future."
