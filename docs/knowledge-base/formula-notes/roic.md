# Return on invested capital (ROIC)

Source: Investment Analysis And Portfolio Management.pdf, page 553,
equation 15.8. Total capital is described on page 327.

## Formula

ROIC = EBIT * (1 - tax_rate) / total_capital

## Symbols

- EBIT: earnings before interest and taxes.
- tax_rate: the firm's tax rate.
- total_capital: all the capital in the enterprise: debt, preferred
  stock, and common stock.

## Quote

Page 553: "ROIC = EBIT (1 – Tax Rate)/Total Capital"

Page 327: "all the capital involved in the enterprise (debt, preferred stock,
and common stock)"
